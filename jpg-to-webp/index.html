Why convert JPG to WebP?

WebP is a modern format from Google that is typically 25–35% smaller than JPG at the same quality, which makes websites load faster.

Our converter decodes the JPG image and re-encodes it as WebP directly on your device, so the conversion is quick and your images stay private.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert JPG to WebP?

Upload your JPG images, make sure WebP is selected and click Convert. Your WebP files are ready to download immediately.

Is there a file limit?

No. You can convert as many files as you want, completely free.

Will I lose quality?

At the default 92% quality the difference is not visible. You can raise the quality slider up to 100%.

What happens to transparent backgrounds?

WebP supports transparency, so transparent areas are kept.
