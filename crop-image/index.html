How to crop an image

  1. Upload the image you want to crop.
  2. Drag the box to move it and pull the corners to resize it.
  3. Choose an aspect ratio (1:1, 4:3, 16:9…) or type the exact width and height.
  4. Click "Crop & download".

Frequently asked questions

How do I crop a photo into a square?

Choose the 1:1 ratio, position the box and click Crop.

Does cropping reduce quality?

No. The cropped area keeps the original pixels; only the parts outside the box are removed.

Can I crop on my phone?

Yes. You can drag the box and handles with your finger.
