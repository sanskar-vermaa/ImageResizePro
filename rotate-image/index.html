How to rotate an image

Frequently asked questions

Why is my photo sideways?

Phones store the camera orientation separately. Some apps ignore it, so the picture appears rotated. Rotating it here saves the pixels in the correct direction.

Can I rotate by a custom angle?

Yes — open Advanced options and use the Custom angle slider to straighten a tilted horizon.
