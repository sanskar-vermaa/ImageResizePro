Frequently asked questions

How do I put two photos side by side?

Upload both photos and keep "Side by side" selected. They are scaled to the same height automatically.

Can I merge front and back of an ID card?

Yes — choose "Stacked" to put the front above the back in one image, which many forms ask for.
