Frequently asked questions

Which format should I choose?

JPG for photos that must work everywhere, PNG for graphics or transparency, WebP for the smallest files on websites, and ICO for favicons.

Can I convert HEIC iPhone photos?

Only if your browser can open HEIC (Safari on Mac and iPhone can). Otherwise set your iPhone camera to "Most Compatible" to save JPG.

Is it free?
