How to create a favicon

  1. Upload your logo — a square PNG with a transparent background works best.
  2. Choose a transparent or coloured background and the shape.
  3. Click "Download favicon package".
  4. Unzip the files into your website’s root folder and paste the HTML code into the <head>.

Frequently asked questions

What sizes are included?

favicon.ico (16, 32, 48 px), favicon-16x16.png, favicon-32x32.png, apple-touch-icon.png (180 px), and Android icons at 192 and 512 px.

Do I still need favicon.ico?

Yes — some browsers and tools still request /favicon.ico, so the package includes it alongside the PNG icons.
