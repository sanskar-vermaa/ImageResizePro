How to flip an image

  1. Upload your image(s).
  2. Tick "Flip horizontally" to mirror, or "Flip vertically" to turn upside down.
  3. Click "Flip images" and download.

Frequently asked questions

How do I un-mirror a selfie?

Upload it and keep "Flip horizontally" ticked. The text and your face will be shown the right way round.

Is quality lost when flipping?

No, flipping simply rearranges the pixels.
